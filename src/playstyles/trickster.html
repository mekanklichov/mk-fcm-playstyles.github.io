Pro Gameplay Tip

Touch Controls Pro-Tip

Chains skill moves without animation delay, making 1v1 wing isolation almost unguardable for defenders. The seamless transitions between moves keep opposition full-backs off balance and unable to time tackles. It allows creative dribblers to carve out pathing down the flanks with ease.

When Activated

Performing skill moves to beat defenders.
Gesture Execution

How to Perform

Touch Input: Tap or swipe sprint button up/down/left/right when possessing.
