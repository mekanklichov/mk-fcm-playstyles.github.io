1. OVERVIEW & TIERS
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Unlike console editions, EA SPORTS FC Mobile features 22 distinct PlayStyles built specifically for touch controls and mobile pitch mechanics.

PlayStyles are split into three performance levels:
- Level 1 (Silver): Provides base enhancements to passing, shooting, physicality, or defensive actions.
- Level 2 (Gold): Unlocks maximum animation speed, extended range, and secondary effects (e.g., causing opponents to stumble).

3. DETAILED BREAKDOWN BY CATEGORY
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[FINISHING]

1. Finesse Expert
   - Player can curl finesse shots past the keeper with great accuracy.
   - Silver (Lvl 1): Performs finesse shots with slight faster ball speed, additional curve and improved accuracy.
   - Gold (Lvl 2): Performs finesse shots with greater ball speed, additional curve and improved accuracy.
   - Violet (Lvl 3): Performs finesse shots much with even greater ball speed, additional curve and improved accuracy.

2. Clinical Finisher
   - Player can accurately place the ball in the net from close to mid-range shooting distance.
   - Silver (Lvl 1): Increases accuracy and shot speed for regular shots from mid-to close range.
   - Gold (Lvl 2): Further increases accuracy and shot speed for regular shots from mid-to close range.
   - Violet (Lvl 3): Exceptionally increases accuracy and shot speed for regular shots from mid-to close range.

3. Power Shot
   - Player can take powerful shots with high precision
   - Silver (Lvl 1): Increases accuracy and shot speed for power shots.
   - Gold (Lvl 2): Further increases accuracy and shot speed for power shots, while also providing faster kick animation and lower elevation to keep shot on target.
   - Violet (Lvl 3): Greatly increases accuracy and shot speed for power shots, while also providing even faster kick animation and lower elevation to keep shot on target.

4. Penalty Expert
   - Player has exceptional accuracy at penalty kicks.
   - Silver (Lvl 1): Increases accuracy and shot speed for penalty kicks.
   - Gold (Lvl 2): Further increases accuracy and shot speed for penalty kicks, while also showing a red dot of the kick target after your swipe.
   - Violet (Lvl 3): Greatly increases accuracy and shot speed for penalty kicks, while also showing a red dot of the kick target after your swipe.

5. Chip Shot
   - Player has exceptional skill at chipping the goalkeeper.
   - Silver (Lvl 1): Chip shots have an unique dipping trajectory with increased accuracy and shot speed.
   - Gold (Lvl 2): Chip shots have an unique dipping trajectory with further increased accuracy and shot speed.
   - Violet (Lvl 3): Chip shots have an unique dipping trajectory with greatly increased accuracy and shot speed.

6. Dead ball 
   - Player who is known for performing direct free kick shooting with great accuracy.
   - Silver (Lvl 1): Increases accuracy and shot speed for free kick shots.
   - Gold (Lvl 2): Further increases accuracy and shot speed, plus enhanced trajectory curvature for free kick shots at long range, while also showing a red dot of the kick target after your swipe.
   - Violet (Lvl 3):Also applies a new shot type that allows the ball to travel at high speed with sudden dips and unpredictable movement.


[BALL CONTROL]

7. Trickster
   - Player can more effectively perform skill moves and beat defenders in 1v1 situations.
   - Silver (Lvl 1): Performs skill moves with increased speed while keeping the ball close.
   - Gold (Lvl 2): Performs skill moves with even more speed while keeping the ball close.
   - Violet (Lvl 3): Performs skill moves with much more speed while staying tight on the ball.

8. Rapid
   - Player has a more explosive acceleration for sprint dribble and knock-ons.
   - Silver (Lvl 1): Faster acceleration when sprint dribbling.
   - Gold (Lvl 2): Even faster acceleration when sprint dribbling and knock-ons, with better ball control ability.
   - Violet (Lvl 3): Super faster acceleration when sprint dribbling and knock-ons, with even better ball control ability.

[PASSING]

9. Tiki Taka
   - Player is able to perform more accurate and faster ground passes.
   - Silver (Lvl 1): Ground passes are faster and highly accurate.
   - Gold (Lvl 2): Ground passes & dinked passes are even faster and more accurate.
   - Violet (Lvl 3): Ground passes & dinked passes are significantly faster and accurate, with reduced trap error of the receiver.

10. Bullet Pass
   - Player is able to perform more accurate and faster driven ground passes.
   - Silver (Lvl 1): Increases accuracy and pass speed, without impacting the trapping difficulty of the receiver.
   - Gold (Lvl 2): Greatly increases accuracy and pass speed, without impacting the trapping difficulty of the receiver.
   - Violet (Lvl 3): Significantly increases accuracy and pass speed, without impacting the trapping difficulty of the receiver.

11. Whipped Cross
   - Player can perform high-speed whipped crosses into the box.
   - Silver (Lvl 1):  Lob crosses will have increased accuracy and curve. Plus reduced error when taking corner kicks.
   - Gold (Lvl 2):  Lob and ground crosses are faster, with further increased accuracy and curve. Plus reduced error when taking corner kicks.
   - Violet (Lvl 3): All crosses are significanlty faster, with greatly increased accuracy and curve. Also increases corner kick accuracy. Plus reduced error when taking corner kicks.

12. Incisive through pass 
   - Player who is known for making defense-splitting through passes for a teammate to run onto. 
   - Silver (Lvl 1): Ground Through Passes are more accurate, and travel at a higher speed. 
   - Gold (LvI 2): Ground Through Passes and Dinked Ground Through Passes are even more accurate, and travel at a higher speed. Plus reduced trap error of the receiver. 
   - Violet (Lvl 3):  Ground Through Passes and Dinked Ground Through Passes are significantly more accurate, with more impact on user control, and travel at a higher speed. Plus reduced trap error of the
     receiver.

13. Driven lob through 
   - Player who is known for performing accurate lobbed through passes to teammates. 
   - Silver (Lvl 1): Lob through passes are more accurate, also travel at a slightly higher speed. 
   - Gold (LvI 2): Lob through passes are even more accurate, also travel at a higher speed. Plus reduced trap error of the receiver.
   - Violet (Lvl 3): Lob through passes are much more accurate, also travel at an even higher speed. Plus reduced trap error of the receiver.

[DEFENDING]

14. Anticipate
   - Player has a higher stand tackle proficiency and success rate.
   - Silver (Lvl 1): Improved standing tackle range, speed and the ability to stop the ball directly at their feet.
   - Gold (Lvl 2): Much improved standing tackle range, speed and the ability to stop the ball directly at their feet. 
   - Violet (Lvl 3): Also increases chance of triggering opponent stumble or fall.

15. Guardian
   - Player has a higher hard tackle proficiency and success rate.
   - Silver (Lvl 1): Improved hard tackle range, speed and the ability to stop the ball directly at their feet.
   - Gold (Lvl 2): Much improved hard tackle range, speed and the ability to stop the ball directly at their feet. 
   - Violet (Lvl 3): Also increases the chance of triggering opponent stumble or fall.

16. Slide tackle 
   - Player who is known for often performing slide tackles. 
   - Silver (Lvl 1): Improved slide tackle coverage, plus quicker execution and faster recovery. 
   - Gold (Lvl 2): Greatly improved slide tackle coverage, plus even quicker execution and faster recovery. Also grants better ball control when performing a slide tackle.
   - Violet (Lvl 3): Significantly improved slide tackle coverage, plus much quicker execution and faster recovery. Also grants better ball control when performing a slide tackle.

[PHYSICAL]

17. Accelerator
   - Player is able to have a quick burst of speed when accelerating without the ball .
   - Silver (Lvl 1): Accelerates faster without the ball.
   - Gold (Lvl 2): Accelerates even faster without the ball.
   - Violet (Lvl 3): Accelerates exceptionally fast without the ball.

18. Relentless
   - Player has great stamina and is able to make many runs without getting tired easily.
   - Silver (Lvl 1): Reduces fatigue loss during play.
   - Gold (Lvl 2): Reduces fatigue loss during play and increases stamina recovery during halftime.
   - Violet (Lvl 3): Greatly reduces fatigue loss during play and greatly increases stamina recovery during halftime.

19. Bruiser
   - Player has greater strength during physical battles, both at protecting the ball while dribbling or contesting the opponent during a jostle.
   - Silver (Lvl 1): Increases strength during physical battles.
   - Gold (Lvl 2): Greatly increases strength during physical battles.
   - Violet (Lvl 3): Significantly increases strength during physical battles.

20. Precision Header
   - Player has exceptional performance when heading the ball.
   - Silver (Lvl 1): Header shot, passes and clearances are slightly faster and more accurate.
   - Gold (Lvl 2): Header shots, passes and clearances are faster and even more accurate.
   - Violet (Lvl 3): Header shots, passes and clearances are exceptionally fast and very accurate.


[GOALKEEPER]

21. Deflector
   - Goalkeeper has the ability to deflect the ball far, wide and into safer areas.
   - Silver (Lvl 1): Performs deflection saves into safer areas with increased ball speed.
   - Gold (Lvl 2): Performs deflection saves into safer areas with even faster ball speed.
   - Violet (Lvl 3):  Performs deflection saves into safer areas or towards free teammates with even greater ball speed.

22. Rush Out
   - Goalkeeper has the ability to quickly rush out of goal to pressure the opponent.
   - Silver (Lvl 1): GKs will have increased speed during rush.
   - Gold (Lvl 2): GKs will have more increased speed and better auto tackle ability during rush.
   - Violet (Lvl 3): GKs will have greatly increased speed and better auto tackle ability during rush.

- Full-Back (LB/RB): 		Accelerator + Anticipate + Relentless
- Goalkeeper (GK): 		Deflector + Rush Out


5. PLAYER'S PLAYSTYLE OWNING MECHANISM
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Playstyles are pre-assigned to card and cannot be changed.
- Exclusive Box cards (P2W) will have 1 violet + 1 silver or 2 gold playstyles.
- Maximum OVR card will have 1 violet or 1 gold + 1 silver playstyle.
- Maximum - 1 and Maximum - 2 OVR card will have 1 gold or 2 silver playstyle.
- Maximum - 3 OVR card will have 1 silver playstyle

- Current Maximum OVR is dynamic in game, usually rises by +2 or +3 in every 2-3 events.
