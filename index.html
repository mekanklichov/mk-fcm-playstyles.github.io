Card Mechanics & Rules

Fixed Pre-assignment
Playstyles are pre-assigned to cards upon release and cannot be changed or swapped.
Exclusive Box Cards (P2W)
Exclusive Box cards will feature 1 Violet + 1 Silver OR 2 Gold PlayStyles.
Maximum OVR Cards
Maximum OVR cards feature 1 Violet OR 1 Gold + 1 Silver PlayStyles.
Max - 1 & Max - 2 OVR Cards
Cards 1 and 2 tiers below max feature 1 Gold OR 2 Silver PlayStyles.
Max - 3 OVR Cards
Cards 3 tiers below max feature 1 Silver PlayStyle.
Dynamic OVR Progression
Current Maximum OVR is dynamic in-game, usually rising by +2 or +3 every 2–3 events.
