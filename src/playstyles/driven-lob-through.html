Driven Lob Through

PASSING B-TIER
"Player who is known for performing accurate lobbed through passes to teammates."

Level Progression & Perk Breakdown

Silver (Level 1)
Lob through passes are more accurate, also travel at a slightly higher speed.
Gold (Level 2)
Lob through passes are even more accurate, also travel at a higher speed. Plus reduced trap error of the receiver.
Violet (Level 3)
Lob through passes are much more accurate, also travel at an even higher speed. Plus reduced trap error of the receiver.

Pro Gameplay Tip

Touch Controls Pro-Tip

Floats balls cleanly over compact low-block defenses without giving the opponent keeper time to collect. The backspin on the ball causes it to settle quickly upon landing, enabling immediate control for your attacker. It provides an effective alternative when ground passing lanes are fully blocked.
In-Game Trigger

When Activated

Lofts a ball over defensive lines into space.
Gesture Execution

How to Perform

Touch Input: Pull up on the through pass button.
Tactical Role

Deep Counter-Attacking Transition

Devastating for rapid vertical transitions over compact defensive blocks. Pair on deep-lying playmakers (CAM / CM) targeting rapid, high-acceleration wingers running into open pitch channels.
