Dead Ball

FINISHING D-TIER
"Player who is known for performing direct free kick shooting with great accuracy."

Level Progression & Perk Breakdown

Silver (Level 1)
Increases accuracy and shot speed for free kick shots.
Gold (Level 2)
Further increases accuracy and shot speed, plus enhanced trajectory curvature for free kick shots at long range, while also showing a red dot of the kick target after your swipe.
Violet (Level 3)
Also applies a new shot type that allows the ball to travel at high speed with sudden dips and unpredictable movement.

Touch Controls Pro-Tip

Enables heavy curve around defensive walls and unlocks dipping knuckleball physics for unpredictable set-pieces. The extended targeting line gives precise visual feedback on where the ball will travel. This makes dead-ball situations near the penalty arch as dangerous as penalty kicks.
