Pro Gameplay Tip

Touch Controls Pro-Tip

Adds lethal curve to consistently beat high-OVR goalkeepers on curved shots. It is particularly effective when cutting inside from the wing to target the far top corner. Utilizing this allows attackers to convert low-probability angles into reliable scoring opportunities.
In-Game Trigger

When Activated

Performs a curved shot aiming for the far corner.
Gesture Execution

How to Perform

Touch Input: Swipe down on the shoot button.
