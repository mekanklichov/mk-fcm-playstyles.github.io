Pro Gameplay Tip

Touch Controls Pro-Tip

Punishes aggressive, rushing goalkeepers with a high dipping trajectory directly into the open net. The elevated arc creates ideal loft over tall keepers who commit too early on 1v1 breakaways. Timing the release just as the goalkeeper steps off their line guarantees a smooth goal.
In-Game Trigger

When Activated

Lobs the ball high over the goalkeeper.
Gesture Execution

How to Perform

Touch Input: Pull up on the shoot button when facing rushing keepers.
Tactical Role

Anti-Rush Goal Scoring Weapon

Direct tactical counter against opponents utilizing aggressive goalkeeper rush tactics. Pairs exceptionally well with pace traits (Rapid, Accelerator) on counter-attacking wingers and strikers.
