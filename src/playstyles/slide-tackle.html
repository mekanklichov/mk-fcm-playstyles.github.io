Pro Gameplay Tip

Touch Controls Pro-Tip

Expands slide tackle ground coverage while dramatically cutting recovery lag, allowing quick recovery if bypassed. Defenders travel further across the turf to block last-second shots or goal-bound crosses. The reduced recovery time ensures your defense isn't left out of position if a slide fails.
In-Game Trigger

When Activated

Performing a sliding tackle to intercept passes or tackle opponents.
Gesture Execution

How to Perform

Touch Input: Tap slide tackle button near opponents.
Tactical Role

High-Recovery Emergency Defending

Ideal for high-line pressing setups and wide recovery full-backs. Greatly reduces recovery lag when chasing down rapid wingers and executing decisive last-man ground tackles.
